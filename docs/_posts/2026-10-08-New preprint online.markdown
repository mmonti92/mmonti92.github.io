<p>
Some of the work I contributed to in Le Mans is getting out. You can find a preprint <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01357">Here</a>.
The work is titled: 'Domain-engineered ferroelectric BiFeO$_3$ thin films for efficient bias-free THz emission'
It consists in a very nice comprehensive study of THz emission in single-domain and striped-domain BiFeO$_3$. The material shows a very good THz emission, whose effect is enhanced by ferroelectric domain patterning.
